# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want MétéoExpert to be a welcoming, respectful and technically rigorous project. Participants must communicate constructively and avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing and disruptive behavior.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific in reviews and discussions.
- Assume good intent, while addressing impact quickly.
- Protect private data and never publish credentials or personal information.
- Accept maintainers' decisions and use the documented escalation path.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers listed in `CODEOWNERS` or via the security contact in `SECURITY.md`. Reports are reviewed confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary.

This code of conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant and applies to all project spaces.
